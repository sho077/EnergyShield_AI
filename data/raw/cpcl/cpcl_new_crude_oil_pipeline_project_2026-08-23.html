New Crude Oil Pipeline Project

New Crude Oil Pipeline Project

Overview

Replacement of 50 years old 30” crude line with new 42” line amidst the urbanised habitat Aimed at increasing Crude transfer from Chennai Port Trust to CPCL, the new line is equipped with sophisticated modern technologies like 3 LP coating for improved corrosion resistance, Cathodic Protection and advanced SCADA (Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition) & Leak Detection System (LDS) which help to keep the line safe in operation and thus protecting the environment enroute

Benefit: Increased crude transfer to Refinery for a hassle-free Fuel supply to the State.

Commissioned on

Dec 2018
